SCROLL 06 — TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

WANT THE FULL TECHNICAL PICTURE?

Explore technical specifications, optical principles, emissivity physics, environmental installation guidance, and integration standards.

Non-contact pyrometry determines target temperature from -20°C to 3000°C by measuring emitted thermal radiation without physical contact. Unlike thermocouples or RTDs subject to thermal mass lag and chemical corrosion, pyrometers provide millisecond response times.

Single-Wavelength vs. Ratio (Two-Color) Pyrometry:

  • Single-Wavelength: Measures infrared energy at a single spectral band. Highly accurate when target emissivity is constant.
  • Ratio (Two-Color): Measures radiation simultaneously across two distinct spectral channels ($\lambda_1, \lambda_2$). Renders measurement insensitive to dust, smoke, steam, or window fouling.

Aluminium and non-ferrous metals present extreme measurement challenges due to low emissivity (0.05–0.2) that changes rapidly with alloy composition, oxidation, and surface roughness. Tempsens Aluminium Pyrometers utilize multi-wavelength algorithms to automatically calculate and correct for emissivity fluctuations on extrusion presses, billets, and rolling mills.

The Distance-to-Spot ratio (D:S) defines optical resolution. An EDGE C8 with 300:1 D:S optics measuring from 3000 mm distance resolves a spot diameter of 10 mm, ensuring background reflection does not corrupt target temperature readings.

Industrial pyrometer installations near melt shops or kilns feature stainless steel water cooling jackets for ambient protection up to 200°C, air purge collars for lens cleanliness, and optical fiber cables for 250°C ambient survival.

Tap any question button to reveal the answer:

A pyrometer measures temperature by detecting infrared thermal radiation emitted from an object's surface without physical contact. Thermocouples require direct contact and suffer thermal lag, corrosion, and erosion at high temperatures. Pyrometers are ideal for moving objects, hostile atmospheres, and extreme temperatures up to 3000°C.
Choose a Ratio pyrometer (like the EDGE C8) when measuring through dust, smoke, steam, window fouling, or when target surface emissivity changes unpredictably. Ratio pyrometers calculate temperature from the energy ratio of two simultaneous wavelengths, rendering readings immune to up to 95% optical attenuation.
Aluminium has low, changing emissivity (ε = 0.05 – 0.2). Tempsens Aluminium Pyrometers utilize multi-wavelength algorithms specifically calibrated to cancel out background reflections and alloy emissivity variations on extrusion presses, billets, and rolling mills.
For ambients up to 200°C, stainless steel water cooling jackets isolate the sensor. For ambient temperatures exceeding 250°C, Tempsens Fiber Optic pyrometers separate the miniature optical head from the electronic processing unit via flexible optical fiber cable.
Tempsens recommends annual calibration in an NABL-accredited blackbody laboratory (-20°C to 3000°C) to maintain ISO 9001:2015 compliance and guarantee NIST/NABL traceable temperature precision.
