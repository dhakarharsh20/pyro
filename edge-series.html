Installation notes

The M12 threaded mount is standard — fits most industrial bracket systems. For OEM integration, the Micro's compact form allows direct embedding into machine heads. Specify accessories at order time; field retrofitting on a mounted sensor adds site cost.

If ambient temperature around the sensor body exceeds 80°C, specify the Micro (to 120°C) or Core (to 180°C). Lite will drift above its ambient limit without a cooling jacket.

Emissivity — why it matters for Edge Series Setting it correctly +

The Edge Series uses the 8–14 µm wideband. In this range, most non-metals (plastics, rubber, food, textiles, coatings) have emissivity above 0.90 — making accurate measurement straightforward. For these materials, the factory default emissivity setting is usually adequate.

For metals with an oxide layer (mild steel at low temperatures, ceramics) emissivity varies with surface condition. Measure emissivity once under known conditions and lock in the setting, or use the averaging function to smooth variation. For polished metals — don't use the Edge Series; the 8–14 µm band is poorly suited to low-emissivity surfaces. Use a short-wave pyrometer (A Series or E Series) instead.

Adjustable emissivity is standard on all three Edge models via the digital interface or setup key.

Frequently asked questions 4 questions engineers ask before ordering +
What's the temperature range for each model? +
Lite and Micro: −40°C to 600°C standard, extendable to 1,100°C. Core: −50°C to 900°C standard, extendable to 1,000°C. The extension range is a firmware unlock — confirm availability at the time of order.
Can Edge Series connect to PLC, SCADA, or DCS directly? +
Yes. All models support analog output (4–20 mA or 0–10 V) which connects directly to any standard AI card. The Core adds RS485 for Modbus integration. USB is available on all models for PC configuration — it is not a process output.
Which industries is Edge Series right for? +
Plastics, rubber, packaging, food, textiles, paper, electronics, ceramics, composites, OEM machine builders, and industrial automation. The 8–14 µm range suits non-metallic surfaces well. For steel or molten metal applications above 800°C, the A Series or E Series is more appropriate.
What accessories are needed for installation? +
At minimum: a mounting bracket and M12 cable. For dusty or steam-heavy environments, add an air purge collar. For short target distances, specify a close-focus lens. For high-radiation-background environments, a reflection protection tube. We recommend specifying the full accessory kit at the time of order.
